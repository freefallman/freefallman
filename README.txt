Bio:
Howy “Freefallman” Hensen is a seasoned skydiving instructor at Skydive Milwaukee, proficient in Tandem and AFF jumps. A former City of Milwaukee firefighter and longtime CrossFit L1 trainer, he once lived for personal thrills—holding a skydiving world record and earning three USPA U.S. National Skydiving Championship medals. His life transformed during an Ayahuasca experience in Peru’s Amazon jungle, where demonic attack led him to accept Jesus Christ as Savior. Delivered through Dr. Neal Anderson’s “Steps to Freedom,” Howy now devotes his life to fostering relationships and guiding others to freedom in Christ as a trained facilitator. “For God has not given us a spirit of fear, but of power and of love and of a sound mind.” —2 Timothy 1:7 NKJV
