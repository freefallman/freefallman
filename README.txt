## About Howy “Freefallman” Hensen

Howy “Freefallman” Hensen is a professional skydiving instructor at Skydive Milwaukee, proficient in Tandem and AFF jumps. A retired City of Milwaukee firefighter and CrossFit L1 trainer for over 15 years, he once lived for personal thrills — holding a skydiving world record and earning three USPA U.S. National Skydiving Championship medals.

His life radically changed during an Ayahuasca experience in Peru’s Amazon jungle. What started as a search for fun led to a demonic attack. He was saved when he accepted Jesus Christ as his Savior and found deliverance through Dr. Neal Anderson’s “Steps to Freedom in Christ.”  

Now fully devoted to Jesus, Howy serves as a trained facilitator of The Steps to Freedom, helping others break free from bondage and experience true freedom in Christ. He brings the same passion he once had for extreme adventure into guiding people — both in the sky and in their spiritual journey.
